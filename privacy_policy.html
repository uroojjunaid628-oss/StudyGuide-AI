StudyGuide AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

StudyGuide AI may collect information provided during a study consultation, including your name, current country, desired study country, study level, intended program, previous qualification, academic score, English test information, preferred intake, and budget.

When you communicate with StudyGuide AI through WhatsApp, we may also collect your WhatsApp number so that your consultation can be associated with your conversation.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to provide study-abroad guidance, maintain your consultation profile, and manage your conversation with StudyGuide AI.

3. Data Storage

Consultation information may be stored in a database used by StudyGuide AI to maintain your conversation and consultation details.

4. WhatsApp

StudyGuide AI uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with users. Messages sent to the StudyGuide AI WhatsApp number may be processed by the application to provide the requested consultation service.

5. Data Sharing

StudyGuide AI does not sell personal information. Consultation information may be processed by the technical services required to operate the application.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored consultation information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Consultation information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide and maintain the StudyGuide AI service.

8. User Choices

If you have questions about the information associated with your consultation or wish to request its removal, you may contact the StudyGuide AI service administrator.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
