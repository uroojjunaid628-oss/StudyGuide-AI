StudyGuide AI Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 2026

How to Request Data Deletion

If you have used StudyGuide AI and would like your personal information deleted, you may request deletion of your data by contacting the StudyGuide AI service administrator.

Deletion Request

Please provide the WhatsApp number associated with your StudyGuide AI conversation when submitting a deletion request. This helps us identify the relevant student record.

What Happens After a Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, the associated StudyGuide AI student information will be reviewed and deleted from the application's stored records.

Third-Party Services

StudyGuide AI may use third-party services to provide its functionality. Data handled by those services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and data retention practices.

Contact

For data deletion requests or questions about your personal information, please contact the StudyGuide AI service administrator.
